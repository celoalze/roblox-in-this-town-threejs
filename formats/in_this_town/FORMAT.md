# Format: in_this_town (absurd rule, first person)

Premise: "In this town, <absurd rule>." One storyteller (the narrator, first person) tells everything; characters act silently
(anim + expr + props) while the narrator describes them. Standalone story: ends resolved, no "Part 2" card or cliffhanger line
unless the user asks for a continuation.

Length 90-140 s, 45-80 scenes, one narration line (up to 18 words) per scene, a cut every 2-3 s.

Structure
1. Hook (0-3 s): "In this town, <rule>." shown in action. `title`: the rule in CAPS.
2. How the town lives with it (3-15 s): 2-3 quick examples with different characters.
3. The incident: the hero breaks the rule (on purpose or by accident); escalate every 3-4 scenes.
4. Consequence: the town over-reacts (crowd, authority figure, absurd punishment).
5. Twist + resolution: the rule turns out dumber than thought, or the hero's own flaw is the real cause; one warm closing line.

Writing: mark 1 key word per line with *asterisks*; punctuation drives voice emotion (`!` excited, `...` dramatic, `?` curious).
Silent acting beats: reaction close-up, shrug, facepalm, `hold`. Reveals = `punch` + one SFX on the key word. Absurd yes, mean no. Kid-friendly.
Recurring cast keeps a channel recognisable: milo (hero), zoe (best friend), jay (goofy), max (little brother, scale 0.85).

Viral upgrade (lessons from 8 videos with 1M+ views, see formats/in_this_world/FORMAT.md)
- Emotion first: give the hero a person they love (mom, dad, brother, true friend) and let the ending hit that bond; absurd premise, sincere heart.
- Name the hero ("My name is X") and give the family a one-line saying; pay it off at the end with a new meaning.
- Plant 2-3 `clue` scenes BEFORE the twist (a hat, an unused eraser, a bully suddenly nice); never explain them. The twist re-reads every clue.
- Concrete specifics over adjectives ("50,000 timers glow like fireflies"); one big set-piece per act; escalate scale visually.
- Reaction close-ups on emotional lines, `low`/`establishing` for scale, slow `push` into the twist, music drops after it (`sad`), never `none`.
- Optional `beat` tags per scene (rule example hero clue twist cta) turn on the arc lint; end with "Subscribe if you love your <person>."
